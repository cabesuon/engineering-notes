# The weekly structure

Every week has:

### Mission

One sentence describing the purpose of the week.

### Objectives

A small number of realistic things we intend to do.

### Results

What actually happened.

Not what we hoped would happen.

### Reflection

Three questions:

What did I learn?

What surprised me?

What do I still not understand / what should I explore next?

And we explicitly agreed that an unproductive week is still a valid journal entry.
